## 2. TUEG corpus (new, the next big item)

Goal: put TUEG into the pretraining corpus and remove redundant datasets. Agreed (user, 2026-10-21):
- **Corpus design, changed 2026-10-21** (`tools/misc/build_pretrain_corpus.py`, template regenerated): breadth first and a smooth
  hierarchy instead of fixed caps. All 46 datasets restored; cohorts (PURSUE, ErpCore2021, GraspAndLift = same people; BETA, Inria =
  different people) are one budget unit each. Group share = mean of the target and the sqrt(unique subjects) share (motor 19, SSVEP 19,
  ERP 22, rest / clinical 29, cognitive 11 %); cohort hours ~ unique subjects^0.75; equal time per subject inside a dataset
  (`max_windows_per_subject`, new in `IO/dataset.py`); datasets over 1,000 subjects keep a seeded pick of 1,000. Result: 6,089 subjects,
  210.5 h, 40-1,080 s per subject. Paper Table 9 and the corpus paragraph updated. Tiny size floor: one window per subject (8.5 h).
- **Paper (2026-10-21):** appendix section `Corpus construction` (label sec:app_corpus_math) records the math: group share s_g = (1-l) t_g + l sqrt(N_g)/sum sqrt(N_h), l = 1/2; unit hours H_u ~ N_u^a, a = 3/4, capped at A_u (water-filling); equal time per subject through the level t_d with sum min(a_s, t_d) = H_d; nested windows by prefixes of a seeded permutation; rule comparison table. The Pre-training section is shortened (Corpus, Batching and split, Settings), the one-phase schedule is in the method, the masking tables and the settings table.
- **TUEG in whole:** all patients, a thin slice of each (the user wants the entire corpus fetched first, sliced later).
- **Downloads** (TUH NEDC rsync from the 5090, to `F:\tuh_data\`): `abnormal` v3.0.1, `epilepsy` v3.1.0, `slowing`, `artifact`,
  `events` done; `seizure` v2.0.6 and **TUEG v2.0.2 (1.64 TB, 70,841 files, about 2-3 days)** running, with retries. After it:
  check the file count against the server, free `F:` (2,098 GB free after deleting LEMON, Nieuwland2018_N400, Stieger2021,
  Yang2025; `GuttmannFlury2025` kept).
- **To build:**
  1. Compile step with a per-patient minute cap (a TUEG cache of every window would be about 1.7 TB): code, then compile on
     the 5090 or after copying slices here. **decide:** minutes per patient (suggest 5-10) and where to compile.
  2. A TUEG group in `tools/misc/build_pretrain_corpus.py` with its own share. **decide:** share (suggested 15-20 %, taken
     mainly from ERP) and whether `MAX_SUBJECTS` (300) applies to TUEG.
  3. A patient exclusion list: drop TUEG patients that appear in TUAB and TUEV (the Compass TUAB test and the BIOT TUEV test).
  4. Regenerate the template (`build_pretrain_corpus --out configs/Qtome/pretrain.template.json`).
- **decide:** pretrain order. Every ablation below compares against a baseline on one corpus. If the corpus changes first,
  the baseline is retrained on it (3 seeds, 3 SPR, about a day); if ablations run first, they use the current corpus and the
  paper reports both. Suggested: corpus first (the TUEG download takes days anyway), ablations on the new baseline.
